为了与 DBond-m 和 DBond-s 保持一致，本文沿用 DBond 系列工作采用的多标签评价
体系，包括 Subset Accuracy、Example-level Accuracy/Precision/Recall/F1 以及
Label-level Accuracy/Precision/Recall/F1，其中 example 与 label 的定义均与
DBond 保持一致，并采用 macro-average 计算方式。

考虑到镜像肽断裂预测不仅可用于固定阈值下的断裂状态判定，还可用于候选序列
筛选，本文进一步从概率判别能力、概率预测一致性以及候选序列排序能力三个方面
进行补充评价。首先，采用 ROC-AUC 和 PR-AUC 评价模型与分类阈值无关的概率排序
能力，采用 Matthews Correlation Coefficient（MCC）综合评价固定阈值下二分类
预测结果。其次，采用 Brier score 和 Expected Calibration Error（ECE）衡量
预测概率与实际断裂结果之间的一致性，其中两项指标均为越低越好，ECE 按 10 个
等宽概率区间加权计算。进一步地，为验证预测断裂概率能否作为候选序列筛选的
排序依据，本文在 peptide-sequence level 进行 candidate-level ranking test：
对每条候选肽，由其各肽键的预测断裂概率计算预测断裂率 $R_{\text{pred}}$
（Eq.~\ref{eq:rpred}），按 $R_{\text{pred}}$ 对测试序列降序排序，分别选取
Top 10\%/20\%/50\% 序列计算其真实 PBCLA 断裂率 $R_{\text{true}}$
（Eq.~\ref{eq:rtrue}），并以全部测试序列的真实断裂率作为基线计算 enrichment
factor；同时采用 Spearman 秩相关系数 $\rho$ 衡量 $R_{\text{pred}}$ 与
$R_{\text{true}}$ 之间的单调相关性。所有指标均在 5 个 sequence-level 划分上
独立计算，最终以 mean $\pm$ SD 报告。

\subsection{Candidate-level ranking analysis}
\label{sec:candidate_ranking}

为验证 DBond-GT 的输出是否可作为镜像肽候选序列的优先级评分，本文设计了
candidate-level ranking test。对于每条候选肽，模型输出所有候选键的断裂概率
$p_1, p_2, \ldots, p_{n}$，定义 peptide-level predicted cleavage ratio 为
键级概率的均值：
\begin{equation}
    R_{\text{pred}} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} p_i.
    \label{eq:rpred}
\end{equation}
对应地，实验观测的 PBCLA cleavage ratio 为真实断裂键数与总键数之比：
\begin{equation}
    R_{\text{true}} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} y_i.
    \label{eq:rtrue}
\end{equation}
$R_{\text{pred}}$ 越高表示模型认为该肽段越容易碎裂、越适合测序。本文对所有
唯一测试肽序列（去除同一序列在不同 precursor/spectrum 下的重复）按
$R_{\text{pred}}$ 降序排列，并计算 $R_{\text{pred}}$ 与 $R_{\text{true}}$
之间的 Spearman 秩相关 $\rho$，同时在 Top 10\%/20\%/50\% 候选子集上比较
观测断裂率与全集基线的差异，以评估排序的下游筛选价值
（Table~\ref{tab:ranking}, Fig.~\ref{fig:ranking}）。

\textbf{Response:} We thank the reviewer for highlighting the importance of
ranking and calibration for downstream sequence screening. We have expanded
the evaluation framework along three complementary dimensions and applied
the new metrics uniformly to all compared models (DBond-S, DBond-M,
DBond-AF, DBond-AF-opt, and DBond-GT) to enable a fair comparison.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Threshold-free ranking and thresholded classification.}
    We report ROC-AUC and PR-AUC as threshold-independent ranking metrics
    computed from predicted probabilities, and Matthews Correlation
    Coefficient (MCC) to summarize thresholded binary predictions
    (threshold $= 0.5$) in a single imbalance-aware score
    (Table~\ref{tab:discrimination}).

    \item \textbf{Agreement between predicted probabilities and outcomes.}
    We use Brier score and Expected Calibration Error (ECE, 10 equal-width
    bins) to quantify how closely the predicted bond-cleavage probabilities
    match observed cleavage frequencies. These metrics quantify
    probability--outcome agreement rather than claiming a fully calibrated
    probabilistic forecaster (Table~\ref{tab:calibration},
    Fig.~\ref{fig:ranking}a).

    \item \textbf{Candidate-level ranking.} To directly address whether the
    predicted cleavage ratio can serve as a candidate ranking score, we
    aggregate bond-level probabilities into a peptide-level predicted
    cleavage ratio $R_{\text{pred}}$ (Eq.~\ref{eq:rpred}) and compare it
    with the PBCLA-observed ratio $R_{\text{true}}$ (Eq.~\ref{eq:rtrue}).
    Ranking is performed at the peptide-sequence level across all unique
    test sequences ($n = \NPEP$ per fold). For DBond-GT, the Spearman
    correlation reaches $\rho = 0.935$ ($p < 0.001$), and Top-10\% peptides
    by $R_{\text{pred}}$ exhibit an observed cleavage ratio of 0.892 — a
    1.87$\times$ enrichment over the all-peptide baseline of 0.478 —
    indicating that the predicted cleavage ratio has potential value as a
    candidate-sequence ranking score (Table~\ref{tab:ranking},
    Fig.~\ref{fig:ranking}b--c).
\end{enumerate}
